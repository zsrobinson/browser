# AMC A-List booking

Uses the **Home Browser** connector and follows the `home-browser` skill's
consent rules (look freely; confirm before reserving; never sign in).

## Preferences

- **Format:** Dolby Cinema first — large formats are the reason to go instead
  of watching at home. Then IMAX / other premium large formats. Standard only
  if he asks or nothing else exists.
- **Theaters (now, College Park):** check all three — AMC Columbia 14 (Dolby
  + IMAX with Laser, newer Dolby room, often the fastest drive), AMC Tysons
  Corner, and AMC Hoffman Center. Not tied to AMC in general, but A-List only
  works at AMC.
- **From August 2027 (Baltimore, Charles Village):** prefers walkable or inside
  the Beltway. For Dolby/IMAX on A-List, AMC White Marsh 16 is the nearest
  (outside the Beltway); Columbia 14 is the backup. Confirm with him once he's
  moved.
- **Seats:** not recorded yet. The first time, ask what he likes (row, center
  vs aisle) and suggest he add it here.
- **A-List allowance:** check remaining reservations for the week on the
  account rather than assuming a number.

## Procedure

1. **Showtimes:** go to amctheatres.com showtimes for the requested movie /
   date at his theaters (deep links beat the home page). Filter to his
   formats.
2. **Present 2–3 options** in one message: movie, theater, format, date/time,
   and a proposed seat for each. Mention if a premium format is nearly sold out.
3. **Wait for his pick.** A reply like "the 7:15" or "2" is the yes for that
   option.
4. **Reserve** with A-List ($0). If the checkout shows any charge (fees,
   upcharge, a non-A-List showtime), stop and tell him before continuing.
5. **Confirm** what was booked: movie, theater, time, seat, confirmation number.
6. Offer to add it to his Google Calendar (only add on yes).
